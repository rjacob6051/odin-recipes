Air Fryer Shrimp

Description

This is some air fryer shrimp. its only like 400 calories for a pound of shrimp, and its 90 grams of protein! Wow. Those are some crazy macros.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Peeled Deveined Cooked Shrimp
  • Old Bay Seasoning
  • An air fryer probably

Recipe

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 400F
  2. Add the shrimp for 3 minutes to melt the ice
  3. Put a ton of Old Bay in a bowl
  4. Once shrimp is done take it out and toss with seasonings
  5. cook shrimp for another 5ish minutes, tossing to mix halfway through
  6. Eat it
