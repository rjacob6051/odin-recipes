Brown Butter Cookies

Description

Some tasty cookies for when you're on your cheat day. They lowkey taste nutty which I didn't know cause I didn't add nuts but apparently brown butter does that.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2.5 cup all purpose flower
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Recipe

  1. Brown the butter in a saucepan over medium heat until golden and nutty, then cool 15ish minutes.
  2. Mix browned butter with both sugars until smooth.
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Stir in flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt until just combined.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Preheat oven to 350°F
  7. Scoop dough onto a lined baking sheet.
  8. Bake 10–12 minutes until edges are golden.
  9. Cool on the pan 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
